### E3. ⚠ THE R3/R4 OCCURRENCE-COUNT STACK NEEDS A SCOPE, AND THE PLAN DOES NOT MENTION IT

This is the largest thing missing from the plan. § Step 12 budgets `RemoveOccCount` +
`RemoveConfluence` as "the R3/R4 remove-leg count stack" without saying what changes.

**The problem.** R3 says: for an UNTAINTED direct edge `(a,b)`, its multiplicity in
`σ.edges` equals `untOccCount S T a b`, a sum of `edgeOfTuple` over stored tuples' closures.
The bridged write leg materialises edges that are not `edgeOfTuple` of anything, so R3 is
**FALSE as stated**. ⚠ And neither existing guard fences them out — this is the trap:
  * R3-target's guard is `isDerived S (b.type, b.pred) = false`. A bridge target is
    `wAnyNode (c.type, c.pred)`, and `NoBridgedDerived` says a bridged shape is exactly
    **untainted** — so the guard ADMITS bridge edges rather than excluding them.
  * R3-source's guard is `a.pred ≠ BARE`. A bridged-in concrete has `pred ≠ BARE` by
    `isSubjectWildcardUserset`'s outer guard. Also admits them.

**The fix is a SCOPE, not an error term**: every bridge edge targets a `wAnyNode`; every
`edgeOfTuple` targets an `objNode`. So the family gains `b.variant ≠ Variant.wAny`, and the
equations survive verbatim. Discharged for free wherever `b` is a literal `objNode`
(`State.lean::objNode_ne_wAny`, minted for this).

Landed so far: `UsStarWrite.lean::{count_ensureInBridges_of_ne_wAny, count_bridgePre_of_ne_wAny}`,
`Cascade.lean::{count_releaseInBridgesLogged_of_ne_wAny, count_releasePostLogged_of_ne_wAny}`,
`CascadeStrata.lean::count_foldl_writeBridgedOne`, and the scope on `count_writeLoggedRules`
/ `count_removeLoggedRules` / `reachedByW3d2_untOccCount` / `reachedByW3d2_srcOccCount`.
⚠ **Membership monotonicity is not enough here** — a leg that erased one copy of a
doubly-present non-bridge edge would preserve `∈` and break `count`, which is why the `count`
lemmas are separate from the `mem` ones.

**One place needed a new structural lemma rather than a binder:**
`CascadeStrata.lean::reachedByW3d2_wAny_edge_bridged` — *every `wAny`-targeted edge of a
