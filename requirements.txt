# (tests/test_postgres_ha.py, plus the DSN re-runs of tests/test_concurrency.py and the
# connected-store concurrency/multi-instance modules). Everything else runs on stdlib

# tests/conftest.py drops that module at COLLECTION rather than skipping it (verify.sh

# backends -- see docs/architecture/overview.md, "Supported database backends".
